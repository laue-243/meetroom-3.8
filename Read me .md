# Meetroom

Meetroom lets up to 10 people to meet by video, share their screen, chat, send files up to 2 GB, and edit code live,
all from a room. No accounts, no installs.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [meetroom-3.vercel.app]
(https://meetroom-3.vercel.app)
