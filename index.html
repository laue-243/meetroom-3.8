Features

Everything runs in the browser, and people connect directly to each other.

🎥 Video and audioSee and hear everyone. Mute yourself and turn your camera on or off any time.
🖥 Screen sharingShare your screen, a window or a browser tab with one click.
💬 ChatText chat for the whole room, with join and leave notices.
📎 File sharingSend files up to 2 GB to people in the room. They accept first, and nothing is stored.
👨‍💻 Live code editorThe editor behind VS Code, shared live with colored cursors, error underlines and formatting.
🛡 Host controlsThe room creator can mute people, stop video, remove and ban, and limit what guests can turn on.
🎨 Light and darkSystem, light or dark theme, accent colors and text sizes.
⚙ SettingsChoose microphone, camera and speaker, video quality, and editor options.
🔒 Private by designCalls go directly between browsers over encrypted connections. No recordings, no history.
